This survey is a work of original fan architecture inspired by the Warhammer 40,000 setting. No official artwork is reproduced. All structures are procedurally generated from a fixed archive seed and will be identical on every access.

    
The engravings visible far behind the city are public-domain historical plates by Piranesi, Doré, Agricola, Kircher, Boullée, Meryon, Martin and Sant'Elia. 

    
The Administratum reminds surveyors that curiosity below -30,000 m is not a sanctioned activity.
