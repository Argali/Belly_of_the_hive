## What this project contains

- **No Games Workshop artwork.** Every visual element is generated at runtime by
  `js/architecture.js` and `js/engraving.js`. There are no bitmaps of any kind in
  this repository.
- **No Games Workshop logos or trade dress.**
- **No text copied from Games Workshop publications.** The lore in `data/terra.json`
  is original writing set in a world Games Workshop owns.
- **No money.** This is free, has no ads, no sponsorship, no donation link, no
  paid tier, and is not to be monetised by anyone.

## Compliance with Games Workshop's IP Guidelines

Games Workshop's published guidelines tolerate non-commercial fan fiction and hobby
material provided it takes no money, is distributed only as a free digital work, is
clearly marked unofficial, and uses no Games Workshop logos. This project is intended
to sit inside those limits. Their guidelines are explicit that they do not constitute
approval, are not a waiver of any right, and may change at any time.

If Games Workshop ask for this to come down, it comes down. No argument.

## Public-domain material

The optional historical plates that `tools/fetch-plates.mjs` can download are public
domain worldwide — every artist died more than 70 years ago and the works were
published between 1556 and 1914. They are not redistributed in this repository. Full
attribution is in `data/plates.json`, in the README, and in the application itself
under the PLATES panel.
